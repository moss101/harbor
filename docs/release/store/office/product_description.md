# Harbor Office Suite — store description

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Office that never leaves your device: sheets, docs, decks and PDF.

## Long description
Harbor Office Suite is a complete, private office suite for your phone
and tablet. Everything runs on your device — no account, no cloud, no
tracking. Your files never leave your hands.

SPREADSHEETS
Open and edit .xlsx workbooks: type values and formulas, insert and
delete rows and columns (references shift the way Excel shifts them),
bold headers, add charts, highlight cells above a threshold, and switch
between sheets. 99 formula targets are qualified across three platform
tiers, and formula results are recalculated on-device — a cached value
is never shown as verified.

DOCUMENTS
Open .docx files, read them with a proper typographic outline, and edit:
long-press any paragraph to change its text or turn it into a heading,
a bullet or a numbered list. Create new documents from scratch.

PRESENTATIONS & PDF
View .pptx decks with a filmstrip navigator, and edit slide titles and
bullets (everything else stays untouched, byte-for-byte). Open PDFs and
extract their text. Convert Markdown to Word or PowerPoint; convert
Word and spreadsheets to PDF; print through the system print dialog.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-in. No analytics. No network use at all — the suite performs no
online sessions. Files are imported into the app's own store so they
stay available offline, and unsaved edits are automatically recovered
if the app closes. Full English and Arabic interfaces, with dark mode.

IMPORT/EXPORT: .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf, .md — plus "Open in Harbor
Office Suite" from Files, Mail and other apps.
