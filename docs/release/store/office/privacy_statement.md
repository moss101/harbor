# Harbor Office Suite — privacy statement

Harbor Office Suite works entirely on your device.

- The app performs NO network requests. It holds no internet
  permission on Android beyond the shared app template; on iOS it makes
  no connections. There is no account, no sign-in, no telemetry, no
  analytics and no advertising.
- Documents you open or create are imported into the app's own storage.
  On iOS that store is reachable from Files (user-visible by design);
  on Android it is app-private.
- Unsaved edits are auto-saved to a recovery draft inside the app's own
  storage, and are cleared the moment you save.
- The encrypted diagnostics log the app keeps for crash reports is
  exported only by you, manually, from Settings.
- Printing hands the rendered document to the SYSTEM print dialog; the
  app itself sends nothing anywhere.

In short: files in, files out, nothing phoned home.
