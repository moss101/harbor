# Harbor Office Suite — FAQ

**Does it need an account or the internet?**
No. The suite is fully offline. It makes no network connections at all.

**Can it open my existing files?**
Yes — .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf and .md. Use Open file, or share the
file into "Open in Harbor Office Suite" from Files, Mail or any app.

**Are formulas supported?**
Yes. 99 formula targets are qualified (arithmetic, lookups, text, date,
statistics and more), recalculated on device. Where a workbook uses
something outside the qualified set, the app tells you honestly which
cells are unverified instead of guessing.

**Can I edit Word documents?**
Yes — paragraph text and styles (headings, bullet and numbered lists).
Tables' cell text can be edited as paragraphs.

**Can I edit PowerPoint files?**
Slide titles and bullets. Everything else in the file is preserved
byte-for-byte and left untouched.

**How do I print or make a PDF?**
The printer icon opens the system print dialog. Export PDF produces a
PDF from documents (styles-aware) and spreadsheets (aligned grid).

**What happens if the app closes with unsaved edits?**
They are restored automatically on the next launch; Save a copy writes
them where you choose. Undo and redo are one tap away in the toolbar.

**Which languages?**
English and Arabic today (interface mirrored right-to-left).
